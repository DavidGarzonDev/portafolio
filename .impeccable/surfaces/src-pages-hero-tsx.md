# Portada (home)

Scope: the home route, first viewport and the band below it. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio: the work leads).

Audience: recruiters (Colombia and remote) and clients needing custom software, equally. Arrive from LinkedIn, often on a phone, decide in seconds.
Action: write to David (LinkedIn or email). CV download and project browsing support it.
Proof: four real projects in production (Conectate OSS, Sitio web de Conéctate, Gessat, Pizzatelo).
Constraints: black and white only; pixel mascot stays; ES/EN; no invented claims; no copyrighted characters; no contact page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a Cali woodtype show bill announcing its run: David's name billed huge, his projects as the lineup, "Escríbeme" as the closing line. It refuses the developer-portfolio default of a centered name over a row of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Reverse-printed bill: paper-white ink (#efece4) on near-black stock (#0b0b0a), one mid grey (#8c8a84) for small print. Condensed gothic wood type (Big Shoulders Display, heavy) for stacked lines, fat slab (Ultra) for billing words. Ink texture with worn edges and voids; thin printer's rules and ornaments between bands; inverted bands for emphasis. No cards, no glass, no gradients.

STORY: The visitor reads who is billed, sees four real productions on the bill, and knows the one thing to do: write to him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One bill fills the viewport inside a ruled border. "DAVID GARZÓN" set full-width as the headline. Inverted bar: role. "PRESENTA" in slab. Four project lines sized by billing, each a link to its project. Bottom inverted band: "ESCRÍBEME" as the primary action with LinkedIn, email and CV beside it, and the printer's imprint (place and year) under the bill. Mascot walks the top edge.

FORM: Cartel de imprenta de Cali (woodtype show bill), position 5 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:bda1ad49a25f1135. Signature interaction: lines stamp in one by one like a press; hovering a line inks it in reverse. Motion grammar: short press strokes, no floating.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
